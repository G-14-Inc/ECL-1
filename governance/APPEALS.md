# ECL-1 appeals policy

A decision under the governance of ECL-1 can be appealed: first to the chair,
then to a public issue ([CHARTER](CHARTER.md), section 8).

## Appealable decisions

Technical interpretations, dispositions of comments and proposals, registry
allocations, change classifications, errata dispositions, listings and
removals in the implementers registry, and procedural decisions may be
appealed by a materially affected party. A statement by which the maintainer
overrules a recorded consensus for a legal or a security reason may be
appealed on the ground that the reason does not hold.

## Filing

An appeal identifies the decision, the appellant's standing, the remedy
requested, the alleged error, the supporting evidence, its urgency, any
conflicts, and any security-sensitive material. It is filed within 30 days of
the record of the decision. An appeal is not a forum to relitigate unrelated
requirements.

## First: the chair

The appellant writes to the chair, at the working group's contact address
([WORKING_GROUP](../community/WORKING_GROUP.md)). The chair reviews the record
of the decision, may ask the editors and the appellant for more, and answers
in writing, with reasons, within 30 days. If the chair took part in the
decision appealed, the answer says so.

## Then: a public issue

If the chair's answer does not settle the appeal, the appellant may, within
30 days of the answer, open a public issue in the repository with the "Appeal
a decision" issue form. The issue stays open for comment for at least 14 days.
The chair and the editors then give their final answer in the issue, with
reasons that respond to each argument raised there. Where the rough consensus
of the comments in the issue supports the appeal, the decision is changed as
that consensus supports, and the maintainer may not overrule it except as the
charter allows.

An appeal that concerns embargoed security detail stays private until the
embargo ends. The chair answers it, and its record is published with the
disclosure ([SECURITY](SECURITY.md)).

## Standard of review

The chair and the editors evaluate:

- consistency with the released normative text and governance policies;
- completeness and reproducibility of evidence;
- compatibility, security, safety, and reliance interests;
- procedural fairness and disclosed conflicts;
- whether the requested remedy exceeds what this policy can grant.

## Interim effect

An appeal does not automatically authorize disputed behavior. If continued
operation could widen authority, produce false assurance, or create
irreversible harm, the stricter disposition remains in force. The chair may
order a bounded temporary hold with a recorded rationale.

## Decision record

Each written answer states who decided, the disclosures, the issues, the
evidence, the reasoning, the outcome, the remedy, the effective date, and any
release, reassessment, registry, or errata action. Dissent may be recorded. A
decision cannot rewrite historical release evidence. In a public review, the
answer is also recorded in the disposition log, with `chair` as the decider
([PUBLIC_REVIEW](PUBLIC_REVIEW.md)).

## Finality

The answer in the public issue is final under this policy. One reconsideration
is available for material new evidence or a procedural defect. External legal,
regulatory, contractual, or accreditation remedies are outside this policy and
remain unaffected.
