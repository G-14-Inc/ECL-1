# ECL-1 participation code

This code applies in every space of the ECL-1 Working Group: the issues,
pull requests and discussions of the public repository, the working sessions,
the public reviews and the working group's contact address. The maintainer,
G-14, Inc., operates it.

Participants must engage honestly, professionally, and without harassment,
threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, retaliation, or deliberate disruption.
Technical disagreement, security criticism, failing assessment results, and
good-faith challenge to authority are protected participation when expressed
professionally.

These spaces may not be used to:

- conceal material conflicts or falsify evidence;
- pressure assessors or reviewers to change a result;
- publish private vulnerability details contrary to an active embargo;
- expose personal, customer, credential, or protected data;
- impersonate G-14, the chair, an editor, an assessor, a regulator, or a
  certification authority.

Conduct reports go privately to the working group's contact address,
ecl@g14.ai, and the chair handles them; a report about the chair is handled
by a person whom the maintainer names and who is not the chair. Enforcement
actions are proportionate, documented, and may include warning, content
restriction, temporary exclusion, or permanent exclusion. The subject receives
the allegation and an opportunity to respond unless immediate action is needed
to prevent harm. An action can be appealed under `APPEALS.md`; when the chair
took the action, the person whom the maintainer names in the chair's place
hears the first stage of the appeal.

This policy governs participation, not technical truth. It cannot be used to
suppress a substantiated security, conformance, or governance finding.
