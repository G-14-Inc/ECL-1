# ECL-1 patent pledge

G-14, Inc. makes this pledge so that anyone can implement ECL-1 Version 1.0
and its companion specifications without paying royalties for, or asking
permission under, the patent claims of G-14 that implementing them makes
unavoidable.

- Made by: G-14, Inc.
- In effect: from the publication of ECL-1 Version 1.0 by G-14, Inc.
- Legal review: G-14 confirms completion of its legal review of the licenses
  and this pledge. Section 11 governs later revisions; they may not narrow
  the pledge.

## 1. The pledge

G-14 irrevocably covenants, subject only to section 4, that it will not
assert any of its Essential Claims against any person or entity for making,
having made, using, selling, offering for sale, importing or otherwise
distributing a Covered Implementation, to the extent of that Covered
Implementation.

The pledge is royalty-free, worldwide and non-exclusive. It requires no fee,
registration, notice, agreement, certification or conformance listing, and
no use of any product or service of G-14. It extends to the customers,
distributors and users of a Covered Implementation for their use and
distribution of it.

## 2. Definitions

**G-14**: G-14, Inc. and each entity that G-14, Inc. controls, for as long as
it controls that entity. Control means ownership of more than half of the
voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management.

**Affiliate** of a party: an entity that controls the party, that the party
controls, or that is under common control with the party, with control as
defined above.

**Covered Specifications**: ECL-1 Version 1.0, the External Control Layer
Standard, as published by G-14, Inc., and its companion specifications at the
versions that the version set of Version 1.0 binds: DCMP-2 version 2.0, EBMP-2
version 2.0, and version 1.0 of INTEGRITY-ENVELOPE-1, DISTRIBUTED-WORKFLOW-1,
TIMING-SAFE-STATE-1, EVIDENCE-LIFECYCLE-1, EXTENSION-REGISTRY-1,
TRUST-POLICY-1, DCMP2-HTTP-1, TRANSACTIONAL-EFFECT-1, AUTHZEN-BINDING-1,
STRICT-FAILURE-1, AARM-BINDING-1, ACS-BINDING-1, MCP-GATEWAY-1,
OTEL-EVIDENCE-EXPORT-1 and OAUTH-AUTHORITY-1; together with the errata that
G-14, Inc. publishes for them. A later version is a Covered Specification only
as section 6 provides.

**Normative Requirement**: a requirement that a Covered Specification states
with a requirement identifier and a requirement keyword (MUST, MUST NOT,
REQUIRED, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT, RECOMMENDED, MAY or OPTIONAL,
as RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 define them), including a requirement that applies
only when an implementation chooses an optional feature; and the constraints
of the schemas and registries that a Covered Specification makes normative.

**Essential Claim**: a claim of a patent or of a patent application, in any
country, that G-14 owns or controls, now or later, and that is necessarily
infringed by implementing a Normative Requirement because, when the Covered
Specification is published, no commercially and technically feasible way
exists to implement that requirement without infringing the claim. G-14
controls a claim when it may grant a license or a covenant under it without
paying a third party. A claim of a pending application is included while it
is pending and when it issues.

**Covered Implementation**: a product, service or component, or a portion of
one, made, used, sold, offered for sale, imported or otherwise distributed by
anyone, to the extent that it implements one or more Normative Requirements
and conforms to them. An implementation need not implement every Normative
Requirement or claim conformance to be covered for the requirements it
implements, and it need not use any software, account, subscription,
certification or service of G-14.

**Assert**: to bring, or to join as a party, a lawsuit or other proceeding
before a court, agency or tribunal in any country that alleges the
infringement of a patent claim, or to seek an injunction or an exclusion order
on such an allegation.

## 3. What the pledge does not cover

The pledge covers Essential Claims only, and only to the extent that a
Covered Implementation implements Normative Requirements. It does not cover:

- a claim that is not an Essential Claim, including a claim that reads on a
  technique that an implementation may prefer but that no Normative
  Requirement requires;
- a claim that reads on an enabling technology: technology that an
  implementation may need or use but that no Normative Requirement
  specifies, such as processors and other hardware, operating systems,
  networks and their protocols, databases, general-purpose machine-learning
  models and cryptographic algorithms, and technology that another
  specification defines where a Covered Specification only refers to that
  specification;
- a claim that reads only on informative text of a Covered Specification,
  such as notes, examples and informative sections and annexes, or on an
  informative guide;
- a claim that reads on a reference or sample implementation, such as the
  offline verifier, the sample evidence or an example, beyond what a
  Normative Requirement requires;
- a claim that reads on the techniques of G-14's platform, its own
  implementation of ECL-1, that the normative text does not require;
- any portion of a product, service or component that does not implement a
  Normative Requirement.

The code of the offline verifier is licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0, whose Section 3 grants a patent license of its own for that code
([LICENSING](LICENSING.md)).

## 4. Defensive termination

The pledge ends for a party, and for that party's Affiliates, if the party or
one of its Affiliates asserts a patent claim against an implementation of
ECL-1, or against G-14 over ECL-1. It ends on the date of the assertion, for
the asserting party and its Affiliates only; it continues for every other
party.

A patent claim is asserted against an implementation of ECL-1 when the
assertion alleges that making, having made, using, selling, offering for
sale, importing or otherwise distributing an implementation of a Normative
Requirement of a Covered Specification infringes the claim, whoever made the
implementation. It is asserted against G-14 over ECL-1 when the assertion
alleges that G-14 infringes the claim by implementing, publishing or
maintaining ECL-1.

## 5. Successors and assignees

The pledge binds the successors and assignees of G-14 and of each Essential
Claim. G-14 makes any transfer of an Essential Claim, and any grant of an
exclusive license under one, subject to this pledge, so that it binds each
later holder of the claim.

## 6. Duration and later versions

For each Covered Specification, the pledge lasts for as long as any Essential
Claim for it remains in force, subject only to section 4. G-14 may not
withdraw it for a Covered Specification.

A later version of ECL-1, or a later version of a companion specification, is
a Covered Specification only if G-14, Inc. states in writing, when it
publishes that version, that this pledge covers it.

## 7. Contributors

Each contributor to ECL-1 makes, for its contributions, the same pledge on the
same terms, with the contributor in the place of G-14: it covenants not to
assert its Essential Claims against any Covered Implementation, to the extent
that the Normative Requirements that the implementation implements are text
that the contributor contributed or that its contribution changed. The
contributor's pledge applies to each Covered Specification that includes the
contribution, from the publication of that specification. A contributor makes
the pledge by contributing under the terms of
[governance/CONTRIBUTING.md](governance/CONTRIBUTING.md); it signs nothing
else.

## 8. The licenses

The copyright licenses of ECL-1 are stated in [LICENSING](LICENSING.md). The
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license grants no patent
rights. The Apache License, Version 2.0 grants, in its Section 3, a patent
license from each contributor of code for the claims that its own
contribution infringes. This pledge is separate from both licenses and limits
neither.

## 9. No other rights

The pledge grants no right in any trademark, copyright or trade secret, and no
license or other right arises from it by implication, estoppel or otherwise
beyond what it states. It gives no warranty. It does not say that any
particular claim is essential, and it creates no obligation to file,
prosecute, maintain, enforce or disclose any patent or application.

## 10. Patent status

G-14, Inc. has patents pending. Nothing in ECL-1 says or implies that a patent
has been granted.

## 11. Revisions

The pledge is in effect as written from the publication of Version 1.0.
If G-14, Inc. revises the wording, it publishes the revision in this file
with the date of the change. A revision may clarify the pledge or widen it. It does not narrow the
protection that this text gives, for any Covered Specification or any
implementation.
