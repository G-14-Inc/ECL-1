# The ECL-1 Working Group

The ECL-1 Working Group develops and maintains ECL-1 in the open. G-14, Inc.
maintains ECL-1 and provides the editors and the chair; anyone may join, and
joining is free. The [charter](../governance/CHARTER.md) sets out the roles,
how decisions are made and what the maintainer may not do.

G-14, Inc. and Robert Tilley Jr. provide the interim editorial and contact
function while the community working group is being formed. ECL-1 is open to
community participation and stewardship under the charter. Community roles,
working-session dates and agendas will be announced publicly as they are
established; no outside appointment or completed meeting is implied.
Contact ecl@g14.ai to participate.

## How to join

1. Have a GitHub account.
2. Sign up, either with the sign-up form on the ECL-1 site or by opening an
   issue in the public repository and choosing the working group's sign-up
   form, "ECL-1 Working Group sign-up", in the new-issue chooser. Give the
   name under which you take part, your organization if you take part for
   one, and how you want to take part, and acknowledge the Developer
   Certificate of Origin sign-off that every contributed commit carries.
3. The chair answers the issue, labels it `participant` and closes it. The
   issue stays as the public record that you joined.

There is no membership agreement and no fee. What you contribute is under the
contribution terms of [CONTRIBUTING](../governance/CONTRIBUTING.md): a
Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off on every commit, the licenses of the
files you change, and the patent pledge for your contributions. You need not
join to open an issue, comment or contribute.

To leave, say so in your sign-up issue, or write to the working group's
contact address, ecl@g14.ai.

## How the working group works

- **In the open.** The work happens in the issues, the pull requests and the
  discussions of the public repository, and in public working sessions.
  Anyone may read all of it.
- **Decisions.** The editors propose, the working group discusses, and the
  editors decide by rough consensus and record the reason in the issue or the
  pull request ([CHARTER](../governance/CHARTER.md), section 8). Appeals go to
  the chair, then to a public issue ([APPEALS](../governance/APPEALS.md)).
- **Public review.** Each new minor or major version has a public review
  window, 30 days for a minor version and 60 days for a major one, announced
  on the ECL-1 site; every comment gets a published disposition
  ([PUBLIC_REVIEW](../governance/PUBLIC_REVIEW.md)).
- **Conformance claims.** Implementers declare their own claims, with their
  conformance reports, and are listed in the
  [implementers registry](implementers.yaml) as self-declared
  ([CONFORMANCE_CLAIMS](../governance/CONFORMANCE_CLAIMS.md)).

## Working sessions

- **Schedule.** The working group meets in public working sessions. The chair
  publishes the schedule on the ECL-1 site and in the "Announcements" category
  of the repository's discussions ([DISCUSSIONS](DISCUSSIONS.md)).
- **Agenda.** The chair posts each session's agenda, with the details for
  joining it, in the "Working sessions" category at least seven days before
  the session. Anyone may propose an agenda item there.
- **Attending.** Anyone may attend, whether or not they have signed up.
- **Notes.** The chair publishes the notes in the same discussion within seven
  days after the session. They record the topics, the views expressed, the
  proposals made and the actions agreed, and they name the attendees who agree
  to be named.
- **No decision in a session alone.** A conclusion reached in a session is
  written in its issue and stays open there for at least seven days, so that
  those who could not attend can answer, before the editors decide.

The [participation code](../governance/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies in the
sessions as in every other space of the working group.

## Contact

The working group's contact address is ecl@g14.ai. Write to it to leave the
working group, to send a comment that an editor files as an issue for you, or
to appeal a decision to the chair. Report a vulnerability as
[SECURITY](../governance/SECURITY.md) says, never in a public issue.
