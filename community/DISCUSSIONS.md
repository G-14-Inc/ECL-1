# Discussion categories

The public repository's discussions have the categories below. The maintainer
creates them when the repository is published. Discussions inform the work of
the working group but decide nothing: proposals and decisions are made in
issues and pull requests ([CHARTER](../governance/CHARTER.md), section 8).

| Category | Format | Who starts a discussion | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Announcements | Announcement | The maintainer, the chair and the editors | Releases, public review windows, errata, the schedule of the working sessions and changes to the charter. Anyone may reply. |
| Working sessions | Open discussion | The chair | One discussion for each session: its agenda, posted at least seven days before it, and its notes, posted within seven days after it. Anyone may propose an agenda item or comment on the notes. |
| Q&A | Question and answer | Anyone | Questions about reading or implementing ECL-1. An answer marked as the answer is not a ruling; a question that shows a defect becomes an issue. |
| Implementations | Open discussion | Anyone | Building an implementation, interoperability testing between implementations, conformance reports and the implementers registry. |
| Ideas | Open discussion | Anyone | Early ideas, before they are ready for an issue. The editors move an idea that is ready to an issue with the "Propose a change" form. |

What does not belong in the discussions:

- a vulnerability, which goes privately to the security contact
  ([SECURITY](../governance/SECURITY.md));
- a comment in an open public review, which is an issue opened with the
  "Public review comment" form ([PUBLIC_REVIEW](../governance/PUBLIC_REVIEW.md));
- an appeal, which goes to the chair and then to a public issue
  ([APPEALS](../governance/APPEALS.md));
- a conduct report, which goes privately to the conduct contacts
  ([CODE_OF_CONDUCT](../governance/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)).
